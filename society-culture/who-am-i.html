Who Am I?

Identify the concept, research method or case-study example from progressively easier clues. The earlier you solve it, the more points you score.

How to Play

  1. Start with 10 points.
  2. Reveal one clue at a time.
  3. After each clue, type your guess and click Check Guess.
  4. Each extra clue reduces the available score by 2 points.
  5. A wrong guess costs 1 point.
  6. Correctly identify the answer before the points run out.
